# Sushi Market — Business Description

## Concept
A modern online sushi-market for ordering sushi, rolls, sets and Japanese food
with delivery and pickup in one city.

## Target customers
Home customers, couples, families, office workers, friends and repeat customers.
New customers need a clear offer and guest checkout. Returning customers need
order history, saved addresses, favorites and repeat ordering.

## Main assortment
- Rolls: classic, signature, baked, hot and specialty rolls.
- Sushi/nigiri.
- Sets for one, two, family or company.
- Sashimi.
- WOK/hot dishes where applicable.
- Snacks and salads.
- Drinks.
- Sauces and extras.

## Product presentation
Every product should clearly communicate name, photo, composition, weight,
number of pieces and price. Promotions and popular products should be easy to spot.

## Promotions
Possible offers: discounts, special sets, first-order offers, gifts, promo codes,
seasonal offers, new-product offers and returning-customer offers. Conditions must be transparent.

## Delivery
Delivery is a core business service. Customers should know availability,
price, minimum order, free-delivery threshold and estimated delivery time before payment.
The city is single; delivery zones may have different conditions.

## Pickup
Pickup is a simple alternative to delivery. Customers need clear location,
working hours and readiness time.

## Customer journey
Open site → browse menu → choose products → cart → delivery/pickup →
contact/address → time → payment → confirmation → tracking → fulfillment.

## Guest ordering
Registration is not mandatory. After a guest order, invite the customer to create
an account for order history, saved addresses, favorites and future loyalty benefits.

## Account and authentication
Possible login methods: VK, phone, email. They should belong to one customer profile.
The account can contain profile, orders, addresses, favorites and repeat ordering.

## Order tracking
The customer should see a clear progression:
Order placed → Payment received → Accepted → Preparing → Ready → Courier on the way → Delivered.

## Notifications
Useful notifications concern order creation, payment, acceptance, preparation,
readiness, courier handoff, delivery and cancellation.

## Trust
Show clear prices, delivery terms, working hours, contacts, real food photos,
reviews, restaurant information and FAQ.

## Mobile experience
The service should be comfortable on a smartphone. The most important actions are
browse, add to cart, checkout, pay and track.

## Local presence
Clearly communicate the city, delivery areas, restaurant/pickup location and hours.

## SEO direction
Cover natural commercial demand around sushi, rolls, sets, delivery, ordering,
Japanese food, popular dishes and city/district delivery queries. Content must be useful,
unique and natural.

## Brand impression
Modern, appetizing, clean, friendly and reliable. Emphasize food, price, promotions,
delivery and ordering.

## Repeat purchases
Make it easy to repeat past orders, save favorites and addresses and discover relevant offers.

## Future development
Possible later additions: loyalty program, bonuses, referrals, personalized offers,
corporate orders, subscriptions, more pickup points and advanced customer segmentation.

## Business principle
The service should make the path:
Find → Choose → Order → Pay → Track → Receive
as short and clear as possible.
